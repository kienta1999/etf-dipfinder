Top-3 notes:
- SMH (semis, 10): McKinsey raised its 2030 semiconductor market forecast 44% to $2.3T in September 2026, driven by hyperscaler capex of about $800B in 2026 and $1T in 2027, mostly AI infrastructure. No material substitutes for silicon logic and memory, so this is the clearest band-10 theme in the scan.
- GRID (grid/infra, 10): The IEA's Electricity 2026 report (February 2026) puts global electricity demand growth at about 3.6% a year to 2030, with 2,500 GW of generation, storage and large-load projects stalled in connection queues and annual grid investment needing to rise 50% by 2030 from about $400B. Generation can be substituted, transmission and distribution cannot, which is why GRID outranks every generation theme.
- COPX (base-metals, 10): The IEA projects copper supply falling about 30% short of demand by 2035 (December 2025 statements, following its May 2025 critical minerals outlook), with new mines taking 16-17 years from discovery to production. Copper's conductivity has no at-scale substitute across grids, EVs and data centres, making the deficit structural rather than cyclical.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- The memo reads crypto-equity as rotation, but on necessity the miners' own pivot is the evidence against the theme: about 70% of public-miner revenue is expected from AI hosting by end-2026, so the world is routing around bitcoin mining itself, hence band 3 not a mid score.
- The memo reads gold as rotation with a central-bank floor; on necessity that floor only earns band 5 plus 1 (6), because gold is a reserve asset the economy can function without, unlike copper, chips or the grid.
- The memo treats housing as a rate headwind with the shortage unchanged; on necessity the widened 4.03M-home gap is the load-bearing fact and puts housing at band 10 minus 1 (9), well above its dip ranking.
- The memo groups DTCR with rate-sensitive REITs; on necessity data-centre REITs split from VNQ (8 vs 5) on record-low 1.4% vacancy and 80.4% pre-leasing, while broad REIT demand stays cyclical band 5.
