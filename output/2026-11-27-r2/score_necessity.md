Top-3 notes:
- **Semis (SMH/SOXX/PSI/XSD, 9):** WSTS's Spring forecast (released 2026-06-02, primary PDF fetched) projects the chip market +90% to $1.51tn in 2026 and +27% to ~$1.9tn in 2027, with every region growing. Nothing, including AI, routes around silicon; the only route-around is policy on AI data centers (S&P Global 2026-10-01: NY statewide plus local bans in 40 states), which is why it is 9, not 10.
- **Copper (COPX, 9):** The IEA's Global Critical Minerals Outlook finds the mine pipeline points to a ~30% copper supply deficit by 2035 (2025 edition, via GTR 2025-05-21, fetched), narrowed to ~25% in the 2026 edition (mining.com coverage), with demand up about a quarter by 2040 from electrification, grids and construction. It is the input every other electrification theme on this list depends on, and aluminium is the only partial substitute.
- **Grid (GRID, 9):** The IEA (T&D World 2025-02-27, fetched) says transmission investment of $140bn in 2023 must exceed $200bn/yr by the mid-2030s, while transformer prices are up ~75% and lead times run up to four years. That is a supply shortage of physical infrastructure that every power-source mix needs, so data-center moratoria trim the load forecast but do not route around the grid.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- **Grid:** The memo calls data-center moratorium risk "the thesis question" for grid/infra. On necessity it is not for GRID, since transmission need ($200bn/yr by the mid-2030s, IEA) holds without AI load. It is the thesis question for DTCR and AIPO, which I score lower or flag for that reason.
- **Silver:** The memo frames silver as a rates and dollar "bubble popped" question. The necessity problem is structural: the PV industry is thrifting silver per cell (-19% PV demand forecast for 2026, Silver Institute), so silver drops from the industrial-growth band to band 5 even as the deficit persists.
- **Nuclear:** The memo's "physical uranium hit a record (oilprice, undated)" is undated, so I did not use it. Nuclear's 8 rests on WNA 2026 (1,457 GWe targets by 2050) instead.
- **NY moratorium:** The memo says NY's statewide data-center moratorium was "signed Jul 14". S&P Global (2026-10-01) calls NY the only state with a statewide moratorium, but other coverage described the one-year pause as awaiting the governor. The signing date is unverified here, and it does not move any score.
- **Defense:** The memo treats defense-us as one block. On necessity, SHLD (global, Europe +14%) earns +1 over the US-only funds because SIPRI shows US spending -7.5% in 2025.
