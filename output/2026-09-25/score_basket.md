- **IGF (#2):** 0.37-0.39% fee on ~$10.3-10.6B AUM (indmoney/ainvest, Sep 2026), and its S&P Global Infrastructure index hard-caps any single stock at 5% with ~40/40/20 transport-utility-energy cluster weights (Seeking Alpha, Mar 2026) — a structural diversification guarantee no other infra fund here has.
- **XAR (#3):** 0.35% expense ratio (cheapest defense fund), equal-weight ~40 holdings with top10 only ~38-45% and the largest name ~4.5% (thetanerd.com, Sep 2026) — the only defense basket that doesn't let Boeing or GE decide your year.

## Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo

- **REMX mislabeled:** the memo files it as "rare-earth", but the basket is ~half lithium miners — Albemarle ~7.4-8.2%, Pilbara ~7.3%, SQM ~6.6%, Liontown, Allkem, LAC, Sigma Lithium all in the top 10 (VanEck fact sheet, 7/31/2026).
- **LIT construction flaw:** the memo doesn't flag that Rio Tinto is ~22.6% of the fund with NAURA (semiconductor equipment) at ~6.7% (ainvest, Sep 2026) — a "lithium" fund that is really a Rio-Tinto-plus-battery-chain fund.
- **ARKX weakest construction in the set:** the memo notes thin R/R, but the basket itself is SPCX (SpaceX) ~11% plus L3Harris ~6.5% in a 43-45 name ACTIVE fund (fool.com, 9/24/2026) — concentrated single-vehicle bet plus active-manager risk.
- **Clean trio is not one basket story:** the memo groups ICLN/PBW/QCLN together, but construction differs sharply — ICLN (0.38%, ~$2B, top name ≤9.3%) is far cleaner than QCLN ($394-532M, Tesla-overweight) or PBW ($216-475M small-cap tail).
- **Nuclear trio splits on basket:** the memo treats URNM/NLR/URA as one block; construction says NLR is cheapest (0.56%) with utility dilution, URA is broadest/most liquid (~$6-7.6B, Cameco ~22%), URNM is purest but most concentrated (top10 80.8%).
- **XLY concentration:** the memo's rotation framing is fine, but the basket is ~42% Amazon + Tesla (composer.trade, 9/25/2026) — nearly half of a "consumer discretionary sector" is two mega-caps.
