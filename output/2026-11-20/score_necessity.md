- REMX: Rare-earth magnets have no practical substitute at scale — dysprosium oxide is up 24.7% and terbium oxide 8.1% from their 2026 lows (CITIC Securities, Nov 16, 2026) while the equities sit ~60% off their highs, and the April 2025 licensing regime on seven heavy rare earths was never suspended (MOFCOM/GACC Announcement No. 58, Nov 9, 2026). - SMH (semis): Semiconductors are the one input modern civilisation cannot route around — NVIDIA's Q3 FY27 print (revenue $115.8B, +103% year-on-year, Q4 guide $130B, Nov 17, 2026) shows current demand intact even as SemiAnalysis cut outer-year accelerator capex (Nov 13, 2026). The -1 is for that capex redraw: the slope of AI-chip demand is now contested, even though the level is not.
- GRID (grid/infra): The grid is the physical bottleneck for everything else on this ballot — EPRI projects data centers alone could use 9-17% of US electricity by 2030, up from about 4-5% today (cited Oct 21, 2026), and there is no substitute for transmission wires. The -1 is for pace: build-out speed is set by permitting and politics, not by demand, so the need is certain while the timing is not.
