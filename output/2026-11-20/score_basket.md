Top-3 notes:

- **GLD (10):** GLD holds 100% physical allocated gold bars in London/New York/Zurich vaults, so the basket is the metal itself — no miners, no futures roll, no single-name risk. At ~$145B AUM and a 0.40% fee it is also the most liquid gold vehicle in the run (dossier scan: $3.47B average daily dollar volume, Nov 19, 2026).
- **IBIT (10):** IBIT holds 100% spot bitcoin in segregated cold storage at Coinbase Custody, with no futures or derivatives, so the basket tracks spot bitcoin minus a 0.25% fee. It is the largest US spot bitcoin ETF at ~$56.1B AUM with ~$1.95B average daily volume (btc-pulse, Nov 17, 2026), making it the deepest crypto-spot basket in the run.
- **XSD (10):** XSD's modified equal-weight index caps every name near ~3%, so its top 10 are only ~33% of the fund and no single chipmaker can dominate — the opposite of SMH's 20% Nvidia. At 48 holdings and a 0.35% fee (State Street, Nov 18, 2026) it is the cleanest way to own the whole semiconductor theme rather than one AI name.
