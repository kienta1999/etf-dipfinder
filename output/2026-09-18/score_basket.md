## Theme notes

**china — KWEB 7 / FXI 8.** KWEB (0.69%, ~$5B AUM, ~$336M daily vol) is the theme-pure vehicle — concentrated
China internet (Tencent/Alibaba/JD dominate), which is exactly the wounded sleeve. FXI (0.72%, $552M daily vol,
top-50 China large-cap, SOE/financial tilt) is broader, more liquid, less idiosyncratic — the cleaner *broad*
China vehicle. KWEB is the purer China-tech bet if you want the wounded part itself.

**rare-earth — REMX 6.** 0.59%, $43.5M daily vol. Globally diversified (~30% China / 24% Australia / 18% US /
10% Canada), so the China-export-premium thesis is intact in the basket — but thin liquidity and single-commodity
concentration keep it mid.

**solar — TAN 4 (vetoed).** Concentrated US/developed solar manufacturers+installers; $46M vol adequate, but the
basket can't be clean while its subsidy leg is gone. Theme broken, vehicle moot.

**nuclear — URNM 6 / NLR 7 / URA 8. Cleanest: URA.** URNM (0.75%, ~$2B AUM, 28–30 names, top-10 ~79%, $46M vol)
is the aggressive pure play — Cameco 21% + Sprott Physical Uranium Trust 14% + NexGen 13% — but concentration and
the highest fee of the three cost it. NLR (~0.56%, ~$4.6–4.8B AUM, 25–27 names, $43M vol) is cheapest with a 2.7%
trailing yield, but utilities (Constellation, Vistra) dilute uranium purity — clean structure, diluted theme.
URA (0.69%, $6.2–6.3B AUM, ~60 names, top-10 ~65%, $161M daily vol) is the largest, most liquid, and most
diversified uranium ETF — the clean vehicle. (Seeking Alpha 2026 comparison table; Motley Fool nuclear-ETFs Sept
2026; foreignpolicyjournal 7/1/2026.)

**silver — SLV 9.** 0.50%, $1.03B daily vol — the deepest liquidity on the board after GLD, physically backed,
no contango/decay. Fee is mid, but execution is perfect.

**space — UFO 6 / ARKX 6. Purer vehicle: UFO.** Both charge 0.75% — expensive for the theme. UFO ($549M AUM,
~50 names, ~$8.2M daily vol) tracks the S-Network Space Index: theme-pure space (satellite/launch/ground).
ARKX ($746M AUM, 43 names, ~$13.2M daily vol) is active: SpaceX 7.2% + Rocket Lab 6.8%, but carries defense,
autonomous-mobility, battery, and AMD-style semiconductor dilution — less space-pure, with manager risk. UFO is
the purer space basket; neither earns high basket marks at 0.75% with thin volume. (ETFDB ARKX-vs-UFO; nasdaq.com
space-ETF pieces, mid-2026.)

**clean — PBW 3 / ICLN 4 / QCLN 4 (vetoed).** PBW: 47% micro-cap, beta ~1.8 — junky structure even before the
policy break. ICLN: 0.39% fee (cheapest), $127M daily vol, global, but the subsidy repeal breaks the thesis the
basket tracks. QCLN: 0.59%, only $7.3M daily vol (thinnest of the three) — expensive to exit.

**defense-us — SHLD 7 / XAR 8 / ITA 7 / PPA 7. Cleanest diversification: XAR.** SHLD (0.50%, $6.98B AUM,
49 names, $59M vol): defense-tech tilt (LMT 8.4%/RTX 7.5%/GD 7.2%), beta 0.32 — cheap volatility, mid fee.
XAR (0.35%, $5.95B AUM, 40 names equal-weight, $69M vol): cheapest, no single name dominates (Karman 4.7% top) —
the cleanest diversification. ITA (0.38%, $14.84B AUM, 41 names, $168M vol): largest and most liquid, but GE 21%
+ RTX 17% concentration. PPA (0.58%, $7.88B AUM, 60 names, $36M vol): broadest basket, priciest. (Zacks 1/23/2026;
Motley Fool comparisons May–Aug 2026.)

**utilities — XLU 10.** 0.09% fee, $894M daily vol. The benchmark-quality vehicle on the entire board — nothing
to criticize.

**gold — GLD 9.** 0.40%, $4.83B daily vol — deepest liquidity on the board, physically backed. Fee above SLV's,
but flawless execution.

**housing — XHB 7 / ITB 7.** Both 0.35%, $178M/$194M daily vol. XHB equal-weights 35 names incl. building products
(more diversified across the housing cycle); ITB cap-weights ~47 pure homebuilders (theme-purer). Both clean
enough; neither has an edge.

**india — INDA 6.** 0.64% fee*, $238M vol. Single-country concentration plus a high fee for a beta product.

**lithium — LIT 6.** 0.75%, $20.6M daily vol. Concentrated producer basket; adequate but not clean.

**grid/infra — IGF 9 / PAVE 7 / GRID 8. Cheapest: IGF; theme-purest: GRID.** IGF (0.37%, $10.2B AUM, 76 names,
$46M vol): global infra across utilities/transport/energy, no name over ~5.3% — the best-constructed basket of
the three. PAVE (0.47%, $13.3B AUM, 100 names, $86M vol): US-only, 74% industrials, Deere/Nucor/Fastenal ~3.5% —
broader than "grid," fee higher than IGF. GRID (0.56%, $11.2B AUM, 120 names, $88M vol): the theme-purest AI-grid
play (electric grid, meters, storage, enabling software; ABB 8.4% top) — GRID is what "own the electricity theme"
means, at a mid fee. (Zacks 5/28/2026; indmoney fundamentals pages, Sept 2026.)

**ai/robot — BOTZ 7 / ARKQ 6 / AIPO 5 / ROBO 5 / BAI 7 / QTUM 9. Cleanest: QTUM.** BOTZ (0.68%, $3.3B AUM,
$23M vol): indexed global robotics/AI, 47% US / 30% Japan, NVDA ~13% top — diversified, mid fee. ARKQ (0.75%,
active, $2.2B AUM, $10.7M vol): TSLA 10.8% + SpaceX 8.5% private — manager concentration, active fee. AIPO: fee/AUM
*unverified* (only 289 days of history), $35M daily vol — capped at 5. ROBO: fee *unverified* this run (believed
0.95%), $14.3M vol — capped at 5. BAI (0.55%, ~$12B AUM, $101M vol): BlackRock active, cheap-ish and deep — but
it's megacap AI (Alphabet/NVDA/Microsoft/Meta), heavily SPY-overlapping, so it's a weak *dip vehicle* even with a
good basket. QTUM (0.40%, $5.4B AUM, 88 names, $37M vol, no holding >2%, 5-star Morningstar): the cleanest vehicle
in the theme — diversified, cheap, genuinely quantum-exposed without single-name lottery. (Motley Fool ARKQ-vs-BOTZ
8/6/2026; barchart QTUM profile Aug 2026; TradingView BAI; ETFDB AIQ-BOTZ.)

## Top-3 notes

  none above ~2% (barchart profile, Aug 13, 2026 holdings), 5-star Morningstar — genuine quantum exposure
  without single-name lottery tickets. Its diversification is the point, though it also dilutes the purest
  quantum upside.
- **XLU (10):** Nothing to criticize: 0.09% expense ratio, $894M daily volume. It is the benchmark-quality
  vehicle — the lens's ceiling, and the reason the bar for the rest is high.
- **SLV (9):** Physically backed, $1.03B daily volume (second-deepest liquidity on the board), no futures-roll
  decay. The 0.50% fee is mid but execution is perfect; it's how silver should be owned.

## Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo

- The memo treated each theme's funds interchangeably; this lens picks clean vehicles: URA over URNM/NLR
  (nuclear), FXI over KWEB for *broad* China (KWEB stays the purer China-tech bet), XAR over ITA/PPA/SHLD for
  defense diversification, QTUM over BOTZ/ARKQ/BAI for ai/robotics.
- The memo's "cleaner/stabilizing vehicle" note for ARKX (space) is challenged: ARKX's 0.75% active fee, SpaceX
  private holding, and defense/battery dilution make it *less* space-pure than UFO at the same fee — UFO is
  the purer space basket, even with thinner volume.
- The memo framed BAI as a momentum-positive candidate; on basket alone BAI is a fine fund (0.55%, $12B,
  deep liquidity) but a poor *dip vehicle* — its holdings are megacap AI names the user already owns via SPY,
  so a dip-buy adds little distinct exposure.
- AIPO and ROBO are capped at 5 per the stop rule: their fee/structure facts were not pinned by a 2026 source
  this run, so no buy-grade basket score was awarded regardless of apparent quality.
- Vetoed themes (solar, clean) were scored for completeness but the basket lens does not promote any of them;
  their low scores (3–4) reflect concentration (TAN/QCLN/PBW) and thesis-breakage (ICLN), not the fee tier.
