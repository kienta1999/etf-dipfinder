It is the purest regulated-dividend vehicle in the set with institutional liquidity, losing one point only to single-name concentration at NEE.
- GLD (9): At $144.61B in AUM with ~6.1M shares traded, it is by far the deepest basket here and holds 100% physical gold with no futures-roll decay (indmoney, observed 2026-09-26). The deduction is structural cost: 0.40% annually is four times GLDM's 0.10% and well above IAU's 0.25% for identical physical exposure.
- XAR (9): The modified equal-weight defense basket (~49 holdings, 0.35% fee) keeps its largest position at just 3.32% and the top 10 at 30.91% (Zacks, 2026-08-26) — by far the cleanest concentration profile among the four defense siblings. No single prime dominates, so the war-premium rotation hits the theme evenly rather than through one name.
