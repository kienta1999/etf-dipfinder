Top-3 notes: 2 sentences each on the single most load-bearing fact, with source + date.
- GLD: GLD is a physical grantor trust tracking the LBMA Gold Price, with \$144.43B in assets and a 0.40% fee (stockanalysis GLD overview, 2026-10-02). It is 100% theme-pure with no futures roll and no single-stock risk, so the only basket knock is a fee 4x what the same metal costs in the cheaper sibling trusts.
- SLV: SLV holds silver bullion in London against the LBMA Silver Price, with \$32.13B in assets, a 0.50% fee and about 17.6M shares traded a day (stockanalysis SLV overview, 2026-10-02). That is the purest, most liquid silver wrapper, with no contango decay, and its 0.50% fee is the only flaw.
- IGF: IGF holds 116 names, its top ten are only 38.36% and its largest weight is Aena at 4.98%, for a 0.37% fee on \$10.17B (stockanalysis IGF holdings, 2026-10-02). It is diversified and profitable across utilities, airports and toll roads, but about 17% sits in oil and gas midstream (Enbridge 3.58%, Williams 3.02%, KMI, Targa, Cheniere, ONEOK, TC), which is not grid exposure.
