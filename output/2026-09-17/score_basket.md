## Top-3 notes

**1. XLU (10) — the cheapest vehicle on the entire board.** A 0.08% expense ratio paired with $21.52B AUM and 100% utilities purity; the price of that purity is a top ten of 58.14% as of 2026-09-02 (NextEra 13.16%), which is concentration you can see and measure, not hidden pollution. Sources: indmoney (observed 2026-09-18), Zacks.

**2. GLD (9) — structure is the load-bearing fact.** A physically backed trust has no futures-roll drag, the structural decay that sinks commodity-futures ETFs over time; at 0.40% it is also cheaper than its silver sibling SLV (0.50%). Source: Fool, 2026-04-02.

**3. XAR (8) — equal-weight structure beats its defense siblings.** Modified equal weight with ~44% in the top ten, materially less concentrated than ITA (74.74%, GE Aerospace 19.01% — Zacks, 2026-07-22) and PPA (~55–57%), at 0.35% fee and $5.95B AUM. Sources: Nasdaq, Zacks 2026-01-23.
