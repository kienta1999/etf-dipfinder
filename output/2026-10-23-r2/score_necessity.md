Top-3 notes:
- SMH (semis): WSTS's Spring 2026 forecast (released 2026-06-02, PDF fetched) puts the 2026 chip market at USD 1.51 trillion (+90%) and +27% in 2027, with memory >USD 800B. No AI, power or autonomy theme gets around this layer, which is why every semis fund except memory-only DRAM gets band 10 -1.
- GRID (grid/infra): BNEF expects global grid investment to top $500B in 2026, with large-transformer lead times more than doubled since 2019 and US transformer prices +79% (pv-magazine 2026-02-27, fetched). The IEA's 80M km of lines to add or replace by 2040 (Electricity Grids report, 2023) shows the need is physical and has no route-around.
- COPX (copper): the IEA Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 (July 2026, via ANI/Times of Oman 2026-08-01, fetched; iea.org returned 403) has copper adding ~7 Mt by 2040, the largest volume of any mineral and the largest investment need at ~$310B. It also sees supply gaps lasting through 2035.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- lithium: the memo calls the drop "price-driven, may be cyclical". The price is cyclical but the theme is not: the IEA GCMO 2026 has lithium demand more than tripling by 2040, so LIT stays at band 7, not 5.
- rare-earth: I agree with the memo that REMX is lithium-heavy (SQM/ALB). That is why REMX scores 7, not 8, despite the magnet-supply case (China 91% refining / 94% magnets, IEA 2026-04-08).
- silver: the memo frames silver as rates-driven only. It misses a structural fact relevant to this lens: PV silver demand is forecast -19% in 2026 from thrifting (World Silver Survey 2026), which caps silver at band 5.
- defense-us: the memo treats the move as pure rotation. On necessity, SIPRI shows US spending fell 7.5% in 2025 while Europe rose 14%, so EUAD (9) ranks above the US funds (8).
- crypto-equity: I agree that the miners now trade with AI. On necessity, the AI-hosting pivot is the only thing lifting them off band 3.
