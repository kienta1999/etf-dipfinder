## Top-3 notes

**IGF (10).** The S&P Global Infrastructure index caps any single stock at 5% and splits the fund across transport/utilities/energy-infrastructure clusters, so the top ten is only ~39% — no name is above ~5.3% (Transurban/Aena) (ainvest, crawl ~Sep 2026; Seeking Alpha 2026-08-29). At a 0.39% fee and ~$10.3B AUM with 96+ holdings in regulated toll roads, airports, pipelines and utilities, it is the panel's closest match to the lens-10 ideal.

**PAVE (10).** The Indxx U.S. Infrastructure Development index is market-cap weighted across ~100 U.S. industrial/material names with the top holding (Emerson) at 3.56% and the top ten at ~33% (stocknear, crawl Oct 9, 2026). With a 0.47% fee and $13.6B AUM (indmoney 2026-10-10), it is diversified, cheap, liquid, and holds profitable operating companies — no visible flaw.

**XAR (9).** The S&P Aerospace & Defense Select Industry index is modified equal-weighted, so the largest position (Axon) is ~3.3% and the top ten is ~31% (Zacks, crawl Oct 9, 2026) — the only defense fund in the panel without a concentration flaw. The one-point deduction from band 10 is the flip side of equal weighting: ~2-3% each to unprofitable speculative names like Rocket Lab and Kratos.
