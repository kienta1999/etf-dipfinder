## Top-3 notes

**XLU (10).** Electricity is the one input nothing can route around, and the IEA's April 2025 analysis says data centers alone will account for roughly half of US electricity-demand growth through 2030, with global data-center demand doubling to ~950 TWh by 2030. That is demand structurally rising on a base with no substitute — this is the textbook 10 band. Source: IEA, April 2025 (data-center electricity analysis); IEA Electricity 2026 reporting (2025 consumption ~485 TWh → ~950 TWh by 2030).

**IGF (10).** The grid is the delivery layer for that electricity, and the IEA's Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions report says annual global grid investment must double to more than $600 billion per year by 2030, with 80 million km of lines added or refurbished by 2040 — equal to the entire existing global grid. No alternative to wires exists for moving electrons; policy can delay it but not route around it. Source: IEA, Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions (Oct 2023 report, reaffirmed in IEA Electricity 2026).

**PAVE (9).** Same civilisation-critical buildout as IGF — the US physical-infrastructure renewal story and the AI-era construction cycle — so it starts in band 10. One point off IGF because its constituents are construction/engineering and equipment companies (cyclical build-out exposure) rather than operating infrastructure assets that own the cash-flowing wires, tolls, and terminals IGF holds. Source: IEA, Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions.
