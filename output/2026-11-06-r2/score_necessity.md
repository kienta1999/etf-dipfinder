Top-3 notes:
- **SMH (semis):** WSTS's Spring 2026 forecast (2026-06-02) puts the chip market at a record $1.51T in 2026 (+89.9%) and ~$1.9T in 2027, and its Q2 release (2026-08-06) confirmed H1 at $702B (+102% y/y). AI, robots, power electronics and defense all route through these chips, and nothing routes around them. The only discount is that memory pricing (+250%) inflates the 2026 number.
- **SOXX (semis):** It shares SMH's thesis; the theme demand is identical and the score is copied. This lens cannot separate the semis siblings, so basket and price must choose between them.
- **GRID (grid/infra):** IEA Electricity 2026 (2026-02-06) projects demand growing 3.6%/yr to 2030 and says grid investment must rise ~50%. The same IEA work reports transformer and cable prices and lead times have ~doubled in four years (~128-week waits). Every other theme in this scan (AI, nuclear, solar, EVs) depends on the grid, so no technology routes around it.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- **silver:** The memo calls the drop "rotation" only. On necessity there is a structural demand negative: PV silver is forecast −19% in 2026 on thrifting and copper substitution (Silver Institute 2026-04-15). Silver's industrial need is shrinking at the margin, which is why it scores 5 and not 7.
- **crypto-equity:** The memo says the cause is "unclear". On this lens the BTC-mining need is weak (record-low hashprice in 2026), and what the market now prices is AI hosting, so the theme effectively has become an AI-power proxy and rates 3 +1, not a crypto-necessity score.
- **clean / solar:** The memo says "no dated news found" for clean. There is a dated structural policy fact: OBBBA ends wind and solar credits for projects placed in service after 2027-12-31. IEA had already cut its US solar outlook ~40% and its US renewables outlook ~50% (2025-10-07). This drives PBW 5, QCLN 6 and TAN 6, against ICLN 7, which is global and utility-heavy.
- **rare-earth:** The memo says the KL truce "removes scarcity premium". That is a price matter. On need, magnets roughly double by 2035 (Adamas) and DFARS 252.225-7052 forces non-China magnet supply for US defense from 2027-01-01, so need rises regardless of the truce.
- **nuclear / grid:** The memo calls these an "AI-power trade unwinding". On this lens, IEA (data-centre power ~2x by 2030) and WNA (uranium need ~2x by 2040) are unchanged. A Texas data-centre moratorium (2027-01-19) would move timing and location, not the need.
- **lithium:** The memo treats it as rotation. A substitution risk is now visible: sodium-ion takes ~18% of storage by 2030 per BNEF, and Benchmark expects storage-lithium growth to slow to ~5%/yr (2026-10-22). That holds LIT at band 7, with no +1.
