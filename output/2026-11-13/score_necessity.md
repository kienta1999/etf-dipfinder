Top-3 notes: SMH — McKinsey's 2026 revision puts the semiconductor market at $2.3 trillion by 2030 in its base case, 44% above its autumn-2025 estimate, with a 19% CAGR through 2030 versus a 9% historical average. Datacenter chip revenue alone is projected to rise from $330 billion in 2025 to $1.2 trillion by 2030, which is why chips sit in the civilisation-critical band with no substitute. GRID — The IEA's Electricity 2026 report (Feb 2026) has global power demand growing 3.6% a year through 2030, the fastest pace in 15 years, and says annual grid investment must rise about 50% from ~$400 billion to ~$600 billion to keep up. Over 2,500 GW of generation, storage and large-load projects are stuck in connection queues worldwide, making the grid itself the bottleneck for everything else on this ballot. DRAM — Memory is the binding constraint inside the semis theme: WSTS (June 2026) forecast memory up 250% in 2026, and the dossier records Samsung's HBM as sold out for 2027 with SK Hynix committed through 2029. No AI buildout happens without memory, and no other technology replaces DRAM/HBM at scale in the 5-10y window.
