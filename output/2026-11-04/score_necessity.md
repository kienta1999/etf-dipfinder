Top-3 notes:
- REMX: The single load-bearing fact is that magnet rare earths have no ready substitute at performance parity today, with the US DOE position cited in 2025-2026 research that most rare-earth uses have no ready substitutes, while China refined about 91% of output in 2024 (IEA 2026). Demand for key magnet rare earths is forecast to climb about 7% annually through 2030 on EV, wind and defence motors (Bloomberg Intelligence, March 2026).
- SOXX: The single load-bearing fact is McKinsey's revised 2026 base case that the semiconductor market reaches $2.3 trillion by 2030, up 44% from its autumn 2025 estimate, on a 19% CAGR through 2030 versus a 9% historical average. Server and data-centre chip revenue alone is projected to rise from $330 billion in 2025 to $1.2 trillion by 2030, a 29% CAGR, which is why semis score near-civilisation-critical rather than merely cyclical.
- DTCR: The single load-bearing fact is that global data-centre capacity demand is projected to nearly triple from 82 GW in 2025 to 219 GW by 2030, with AI workloads driving 71% of that growth (McKinsey, April 2025). Goldman Sachs raised its own 2030 forecast on 2 November 2026 to 273 GW from 217 GW, so the physical hosting that data-centre REITs own is the bottleneck asset for AI, not a discretionary property trade.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- The dossier's argentina note attributes the rout to Lula's re-election, which is a Brazil event; Argentina's necessity rests on its own conditions (country risk at 765bp, Morgan Stanley cutting 2026 growth to 1.3% and flagging a likely technical recession, Payden & Rygel October 2026), so ARGT stays band 3 on domestic fiscal and financing risk, not on Brazil's election.
- The dossier groups QTUM with AI/robot; quantum computing has no independently verified useful commercial result yet and Gartner (October 2026) expects over 50% of quantum startups to fail by 2030 for lack of fault tolerance, so QTUM is band 5 while the rest of AI/robot is band 7 plus 1.
- The dossier groups DRAM with broad semis; memory is a commodity-priced, supplier-substitutable segment, so DRAM is band 7 while SOXX, SMH, PSI and XSD are band 10 minus 1.
