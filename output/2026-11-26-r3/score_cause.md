Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- **NVIDIA leadership is out of date.** Section C says Huang "resigned on 2026-11-12" and names Kress interim CEO. EDGAR shows a later 8-K (acc. 0001045810-26-000090, filed 2026-11-23, Item 5.02): "On November 22, 2026, the Board ... reinstated Jen-Hsun Huang as NVIDIA's President, Chief Executive Officer and member of the Board ... effective immediately". Kress reverts to CFO and Harvey Jones stays Chair. The press release says the board received "input from senior levels of the United States government" and is "confident that Mr. Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk". The independent-director investigation (prompted by reports that Huang was "the target of a foreign intelligence effort") continues, and a new National Security and Export Control Committee was formed. The dossier's own section D ai-semis row already cited the House "Statement on Jensen Huang's Reinstatement" (Nov 23), contradicting section C. Net effect: the top-holding governance risk is mostly resolved, so semis and ai/robot move from "unclear" to band 7.
- **The "16 partners" tariff story is not a new escalation.** The USTR primary release (2026-11-25, fetched) covers **14** economies. Mexico and Norway, 2 of the 16 investigated in March, were not determined. The actions are proposals only (comments due 2027-01-05, hearings from 2027-01-25). Most rates re-state existing deal rates: China up to 20% "accounts for ... the 20 percent tariff rate" of the KL arrangement, and Korea, Japan, EU, Taiwan and Switzerland are up to 15%. These replace the IEEPA tariffs that were refunded (First Solar cites "IEEPA tariff refunds received"). Vietnam (up to 35% vs a 20% deal rate) and India (up to 25% vs 17%) are the exceptions. No candidate's cause score is lowered for it.
- **Korea is not "+70% YTD".** KOSPI closed below 5,000 on 11-17 for the first time since Feb 2 and is -45.5% from its 9,114.55 record (6-22) (Herald 2026-11-18). The "rotation" bucket still holds, now on flow evidence (MSCI cap) rather than on an intact YTD trend.
