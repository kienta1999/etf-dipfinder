- `theme`: the group the same headline moves. `asof`: last close used. `days`: price history length (trading days).

- `vs_sma200` / `vs_sma50`: price vs the 200 / 50-day simple moving average. `rs_spy_3m/6m/12m`: return minus SPY return over 63/126/252 trading days.
- `ret_10d`: 10-day return. `stabilizing` = ret_10d > 0. `vol_60d`: annualised 60-day volatility. `dollar_vol`: 20-day average $ volume (illiquid < $5M is dropped).
- `tp_pct`: take-profit = climb back to the 52w high. `sl_pct`: stop = 1.5 monthly sigmas below entry. `rr` = tp/|sl| (thin if < 1.3). `size_1pct`: position fraction that risks 1% of the portfolio at the stop. `dip_low_pct`: distance to the lowest close since the 52w high (structural stop; 0 = on the low).
- `is_dip` = (price < SMA200 or dd_52w ≤ -10%) and rs_spy_3m < 0. `dip_score`: mean pct-rank of dd_z, rs_spy_6m, vs_sma200 among dips (0 = most beaten up). `theme_score`: the theme's best dip_score. `is_candidate`: theme in the 20 deepest.
- `price_score` (computed lens, lenses.md): depth(dd_pctile) + trend(rs_spy_12m) + 2 if stabilizing.
