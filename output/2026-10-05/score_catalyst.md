Top-3 notes:
- **REMX:** DFARS 252.225-7052 (clause dated May 2024; statute 10 USC 4872) bars US defense contractors from delivering NdFeB or SmCo magnets "mined, refined, separated, melted, or produced" in China, Russia, Iran or North Korea from 2027-01-01, 88 days out (acquisition.gov, read 2026-10-05). It pulls demand to ex-China producers, but it has been public since 2024, so it is a priced, confirmed date rather than a surprise, hence band 8 −1.
- **TAN:** The 2026-08-06 Section 232 proclamation (Federal Register 2026-08-11) imposes a 15% tariff on polysilicon derivatives and minimum import prices of \$0.22/W for cells and \$0.38/W for modules from 12:01 a.m. ET 2026-12-04 (whitehouse.gov, read 2026-10-05). It is a dated, agency-confirmed floor favouring domestic module makers, but it has been public for two months and hurts the basket's importers, hence 7 rather than 8.
- **ICLN (best of the band-6 group):** Its flow is rate-driven, so the decisive dates are FOMC 2026-10-27/28 and 2026-12-08/09 on the Fed's own calendar, which are two-way by construction (federalreserve.gov, read 2026-10-05). The 2026-12-04 polysilicon tariff is the only one-sided dated fact touching it, and only through the First Solar sleeve, so it gets no extra point.

September 2026 was a steady country-risk drift (Merval −7.1% in September, country risk ~566→600+) with no election. Argentina has no national election inside the 12-month window (next general election Oct 2027).
