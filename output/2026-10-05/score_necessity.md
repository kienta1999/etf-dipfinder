Top-3 notes: 2 sentences each on the single most load-bearing fact, with source + date.

- **XLU (9):** NERC's 2025 Long-Term Reliability Assessment (January 2026; I checked the PDF directly) projects aggregate summer peak demand rising by more than 224 GW over 2026-2035, 69% above the prior year's 132 GW projection, with winter peak +245 GW. EIA's 2026-09-09 release confirms the near term: record US electricity sales of 4,135 BkWh in 2026 and 4,211 BkWh in 2027, driven by data centres and manufacturing (it also notes a temporary pause in new Texas data-centre projects).
- **URNM (8):** World Nuclear Association's World Nuclear Fuel Report (2025-09-05; fetched directly) estimates 2025 reactor uranium requirements at ~68,920 tU, rising to just over 150,000 tU in 2040 in the Reference case (746 GWe), with a Lower case still at >107,000 tU. Even the low case is +55%, which is why the fuel theme clears the ≥8 bar while nuclear power as a whole still faces gas+storage competition.
- **URA (8):** Same WNA 2025-09-05 fact: uranium requirements more than double by 2040 in the Reference scenario. URA's mix of miners and fuel-cycle names gives the same no-substitute fuel demand, unlike NLR's SMR/utility exposure.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- Nuclear: the memo frames the theme as "AI-power enthusiasm". On necessity the load-bearing fact is WNA's 2x-by-2040 reactor requirement, which holds without AI, so the "reset" does not touch this lens.
- Rare earths: the memo treats the Western-producer scarcity premium as the thesis. On necessity, demand is real (IEA +50-90% by 2040) but rare-earth-free motors (BMW EESM; Tesla's RE-free PMSM, Sep 2026) are a visible route-around, so the theme caps at 7, not 8+.
- Lithium: the memo calls it a "cyclical glut vs demand" question. Demand is structural (IEA >3x by 2040), but sodium-ion entering mass production in late 2026 is the binding substitution risk, not the glut.
- Solar: the memo leaves the "policy credit status" open. Verified: the 25D residential credit ended for systems placed in service after 2025-12-31; 48E/45Y need construction start by 2026-07-04 or service by end-2027; IEA cut its US solar forecast by 140 GW. That makes US residential a subsidy-gone sub-theme, though global solar keeps band 7.
- Utilities: the memo calls the AI power-demand backdrop "intact". It actually strengthened: NERC raised its 10-year peak growth projection by 69% (Jan 2026).
- Price, YTD and date facts in the memo (gold's Jan-2026 peak, UFO "+60% YTD", ICLN/PBW YTD, the IIJA end-Sep-2026 expiry) were not used and not verified; they are outside this lens.
