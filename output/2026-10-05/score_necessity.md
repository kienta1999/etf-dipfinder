Top-3 notes: IGF — IEA's Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions (2023-10-17) finds 80M km of power lines must be added or replaced by 2040 and annual grid investment must double to over $600B by 2030, with 3,000 GW of renewables stuck in connection queues worldwide. That is civilisation-critical infrastructure with no substitute — the definition of band 10. XLU — the IEA's Energy and AI special report (2025-04) projects global datacenter electricity demand more than doubling by 2030 to 945 TWh (roughly Japan's entire consumption), with US datacenters driving almost half of US demand growth to 2030. Electricity is the one input with no substitute and structurally rising demand, so regulated utilities sit in band 10. PAVE — the same IEA grid arithmetic underwrites US infrastructure (grid spend ~$550B in 2026 per IEA WEI 2026, still below the >$600B needed), but PAVE's North American construction/industrial mix is more cyclical than pure regulated grid, so one point off the band: 9.
