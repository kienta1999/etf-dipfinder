Top-3 notes:
- GRID: The IEA's grids work says achieving national climate and energy goals requires adding or refurbishing 80 million km of power lines by 2040 — an amount equal to the entire existing global grid — and annual grid investment must rise to over $600bn a year by 2030 (IEA, Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions, 2023; reiterated in Renewables 2026, Nov 6, 2026). With 2,500+ GW of generation, storage and large-load projects waiting in grid connection queues worldwide, the wire — not generation — is the binding constraint on the energy system.
- DRAM: Gartner's August 2026 forecast puts memory at $837bn in 2026, 54% of the entire $1.6tn semiconductor market, with DRAM revenue up 247% year-on-year, because AI clusters cannot train or run models without HBM and advanced DRAM. The one-point deduction from the 10-band is Gartner's own October 14, 2026 warning that $3.5tn of AI infrastructure spending faces impairment by 2030 if forecast data-center revenue covers only ~42% of the required return.
- SOXX: Gartner forecasts AI data centers will account for more than 53% of semiconductor revenue by 2030, with AI processing semiconductor revenue growing at a 26.8% CAGR through 2030 (Gartner, Aug 2026). The same overbuild risk that caps DRAM at 9 applies here: the Nov 5 SOX -12.25% rout was a repricing of that build-out, not a collapse in the 5-10y need for compute.
