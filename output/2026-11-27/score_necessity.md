Top-3 notes:
- GRID: IEA Electricity 2026 (Feb 2026) forecasts global electricity demand growing 3.6% a year through 2030, about 50% faster than the prior decade, and says meeting it requires annual grid investment to rise roughly 50% from about $400bn by 2030, with over 2,500 GW of projects stuck in connection queues. No other theme in this run pairs a civilisation-critical delivery bottleneck with a quantified, dated investment gap of that size.
- SOXX: McKinsey (Sep 2026) raised its 2030 semiconductor market base case to $2.3tn, about 44% above its autumn-2025 estimate, with Gartner expecting AI data centres to rise from 36.5% of chip demand in 2026 to over 53% by 2030. Chips have no substitute as the substrate of AI, EVs and electrification — the -1 is only for cyclicality and competing architectures, not for any doubt about the need.
- SMH: The load-bearing fact is the same as for SOXX — the $2.3tn McKinsey 2030 market and the AI data-centre share of chip demand — with SMH's concentrated large-cap basket tracking the same structural demand. The necessity case does not depend on any single vendor's leadership, which is why the score holds even after the November leadership crisis.
