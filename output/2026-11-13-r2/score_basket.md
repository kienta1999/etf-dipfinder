Top-3 notes:
- **GLD (9):** GLD holds allocated physical bullion at a 0.40% expense ratio, so there is no futures roll, swap or single-name risk; the only knocks are cheaper bullion share classes (GLDM 0.10%, IAU 0.25%) and the 28% collectibles tax (Motley Fool / 247wallst, Jul-Oct 2026). With $3.4B/day of dollar volume (section A, 2026-11-12) it is the most liquid fund in the table.
- **PAVE (9):** PAVE spreads ~100 profitable US industrials so that the top-10 is only 33.45%, with the top holding ~3.7% (Emerson), at a 0.47% fee (Global X page, Oct 23, 2026; ETFdb). The one deduction is that broad industrials (Fastenal, Nucor) dilute pure infrastructure build-out.
- **BAI (9):** BAI is an actively managed basket of 48 profitable AI-chain names where NVDA is only 4.38% (#5) and the top-10 is ~42.5%, at a 0.55% net fee (iShares holdings Nov 10, 2026 via dossier D; Yahoo Oct 2026; BlackRock page Oct 23, 2026). The fee depends on a 0.10% waiver from a 0.65% gross, which is the -1.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- **korea (EWY):** the memo's "Samsung + SK Hynix ~60%" is the KOSPI index figure. Inside EWY the 25% issuer cap holds the pair to 46.49% (23.7% + 22.8%, iShares Sep 30, 2026), but each name alone is above 20%, so the basket is still band 3.
- **china (KWEB):** the memo and D row call JD "a top KWEB holding". JD is 3.93% (#8) per Trackinsight Aug 31, 2026; the top is Tencent 10.24%, Alibaba 8.65% and PDD 8.26%, so JD's Nov 17 print moves the basket less than implied.
- **lithium (LIT):** the memo's thesis rests on Albemarle's Q3 (realized price +58%), but Albemarle is only ~4% of LIT. The top holding is Rio Tinto at 22.8%, mostly iron ore (Schwab Oct 20, 2026), so LIT is a poor wrapper for that evidence; REMX actually holds more ALB (8.77%).
- **nuclear:** the memo treats URNM/URA/NLR as one bucket, but on basket they split. URNM (Cameco 21.03%) and URA (~23-25%) are single-name Cameco funds; NLR is a diversified utility-plus-fuel basket (top-10 62.7%, CEG 9.16%), which matters given the memo's own point that the Cameco Q3 miss is partly company-specific.
- **semis:** the memo copies one bucket to five siblings, but the wrappers differ sharply. DRAM is an active, ~13-21-name fund holding Korean memory partly via swaps (0.65%), and SMH has NVDA at 20.04% (VanEck Oct 13, 2026). SOXX, PSI and XSD are cleaner.
- **ai/robot:** the D row says NVDA is "~8% of BOTZ". The latest snapshot has NVDA at 7.8% but behind ISRG 10.11%, Keyence and Fanuc (ETFRC Sep 30 / stockanalysis Oct 22, 2026), so BOTZ is no longer an NVDA-led basket. AIPO is a power-infrastructure fund rather than AI/robotics.
- **silver (SIL):** the memo files SIL as a pure silver rotation, but its top holding is Wheaton Precious Metals at ~21.5%, a royalty streamer with heavy gold revenue. SLV is the clean silver wrapper.
