Top-3 notes:
- GRID: The single load-bearing fact is the grid bottleneck itself, not generation: the IEA's Electricity 2026 report puts more than 2,500 GW of generation, storage and industrial projects in connection queues worldwide, with grid investment needing to rise about 50% from roughly $400 billion a year to keep pace. That queue is why grid/infra scores 10 rather than 7 — the world cannot use the power it is building without the wires, and no technology routes around transmission.
- PSI: The single load-bearing fact is the scale and AI concentration of chip demand: Deloitte's 2026 outlook puts global semiconductor sales at $975 billion in 2026, with generative-AI chip revenue approaching $500 billion, nearly half of all chip sales. That makes semiconductors a civilisation-level input in 5-10 years, not a cyclical gadget trade, and justifies 10 despite the PC-driven volatility in the dossier.
- XSD: The single load-bearing fact is the same AI-chip scarcity PSI carries, sharpened by TSMC's June 4, 2026 shareholder-meeting warning that chip supply will lag AI-driven demand "for years," with 2026 demand growth described as "insane" and advanced-node capacity sold out through at least 2027. A multi-year, named-supplier shortage for a no-substitute input is what separates a 10 from a 7, and XSD's broad semiconductor basket owns that shortage directly.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- Crypto-equity "rotation, thesis intact" does not lift necessity: even if the Oct 30 drop was flows, miner equities remain discretionary leverage on BTC (scored 3), not a 5-10y need — rotation is a cause-lens claim, not a necessity claim.
- Comms "break" does not lower necessity: Netflix deceleration and Alphabet/Meta AI-spending scrutiny are earnings/valuation events, while communications/cloud infrastructure demand (data-centre electricity near-doubling since 2024 per IEA) keeps XLC at 7 on this lens.
- Gold "rotation" does not make gold a structural need: central-bank buying of ~850t forecast for 2026 is persistent reserve demand, but it is a store-of-value flow, not consumed civilisation-critical demand, so GLD/GDXJ stay at 6, not 8+.
- Solar/clean "unclear" overstates the necessity doubt: the US subsidy-regime change and Section 232 duties change who wins and at what price, but IEA's 2026 solar growth of ~600 TWh and renewables overtaking coal keep TAN/PBW/ICLN/QCLN at 7 on 5-10y need.
- Space "rotation" understates the structural shift on this lens: Space Foundation's July 2026 $686B figure with "space has become critical infrastructure" supports 8, even though Q3 price action was risk-off.
- PAVE differs from GRID within the dossier's shared grid/infra bucket: GRID is the no-substitute bottleneck (10), while PAVE's broader construction/infrastructure exposure is cyclical and scores 8, not 10.
