Top-3 notes:
- GRID (10): the single load-bearing fact is the IEA's February 2026 finding that annual grid investment must rise ~50% by 2030 from ~$400bn, with over 2,500 GW of projects stuck in connection queues worldwide. No substitute exists for moving electricity from generator to load, so the grid is the bottleneck of electrification, AI power demand and renewables alike.
- REMX (9): the single load-bearing fact is the IEA's April 8, 2026 report that magnet rare-earth demand has doubled since 2015 and rises another 30%+ by 2030, with China holding 91% of refining and 94% of magnet output. The -1 reflects the same report's finding that recycling could cut primary supply needs by up to 35% by 2050, so substitution is a slow but real route around the theme.
- URNM (9): the single load-bearing fact is the IEA's Electricity 2026 forecast that nuclear generation set a record in 2025 and grows 2.8% per year on average through 2030, with ~30 GW of new capacity in China alone. The -1 reflects that renewables plus storage are a visible competing route to low-carbon baseload, so nuclear is civilisation-critical but not the only route.
