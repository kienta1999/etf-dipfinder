Top-3 notes:
- **GRID:** IEA Electricity 2026 projects global power demand growing ~3.6% a year through 2030, with data centers driving about half the US increase, and says annual grid investment must rise ~50% to ~600B USD a year by 2030. No substitute exists for transmission wires — every new data center, EV and solar farm needs a grid connection. (IEA, Electricity 2026)
- **SMH:** WSTS's Spring 2026 forecast (June 2026) projects the global semiconductor market growing 90% to 1.51T USD in 2026 and a further 27% to ~1.9T USD in 2027, led by memory (+250%) and logic (+37%) on AI infrastructure demand. Silicon is the one input no AI system, robot or data center can route around in 5-10 years. (WSTS Spring 2026 forecast)
- **URNM:** The IAEA's Nuclear Technology Review 2026 (Oct 18, 2026) projects uranium requirements rising 50-144% by 2050 while output from existing mines falls at least 50% by 2040. A reactor cannot run on anything but uranium, so this demand is locked in for decades once fleets are built. (IAEA Nuclear Technology Review 2026)

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- Lithium: the dossier leans "break" on carbonate oversupply and China's battery tax, but that is a price/supply story, not a demand story — 5-10y lithium need from EVs and storage is structurally rising, so necessity stays 7; sodium-ion is the visible competing tech and is already reflected in the band.
- Silver: the dossier frames silver as pure rotation, but the World Silver Survey 2026 forecasts solar silver demand down 19% in 2026 on thrifting — the substitutability is real and is why silver is 7, not higher.
- Vietnam: the dossier calls it "unclear, no new dated news", but Vietnam's NSO reported GDP +9.01% in the first nine months of 2026 with registered FDI commitments +76.4%, a structural China+1 story — hence 6, not 5.
- Crypto-equity: the dossier says the thesis is unchanged, but necessity is 5, not higher — miners are fully substitutable hash-rate capacity and demand is cyclical and BTC-price-dependent.
- Space: the dossier's rotation framing is fine on cause, but necessity 7 rests on the space economy at 626B USD in 2025 growing toward ~1T USD by 2034 (Novaspace), not on the SpaceX IPO price action.
