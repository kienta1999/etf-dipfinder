Top-3 notes: GLD is the cleanest basket in the run because it holds 100% physical gold with no single-stock risk at all, at a 0.40% fee and $118.7bn in assets as of November 20, 2026 (MarketBeat). That combination of purity, scale and liquidity is why it scores 10 despite the fee being higher than IAU's 0.25%. IBIT is the same structure for bitcoin: a single-asset spot trust holding 100% bitcoin at a 0.25% fee with $48.3bn in assets on November 20, 2026 (CoinStats). Its only basket weakness relative to GLD is that it is a 2024-vintage trust in a far more volatile underlying, which is a theme fact, not a basket flaw. XSD earns 10 because its modified equal-weight design caps every name near 3%: 48 holdings, top-10 only 32.8% and the largest holding Synaptics at 3.83% as of November 19, 2026 (SSGA). At a 0.35% fee it is the most diversified semiconductor basket in the run, with no NVDA-overhang like SMH.
