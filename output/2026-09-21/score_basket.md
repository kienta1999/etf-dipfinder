## Top-three notes

**XLU (rank 1, score 9):** At a 0.08% expense ratio with roughly $44B in AUM and $903M/day of dollar volume, XLU is the cheapest, most liquid, and cleanest-structured basket in the panel. Its cap-weighted holdings are all profitable regulated utilities (NextEra, Southern, Duke, Constellation), so there is no off-theme exposure, no speculative constituents, and no structural decay.

**PAVE (rank 2, score 9):** With 102 holdings and only ~32% in the top 10 (Deere 3.59%, Nucor 3.53%, Fastenal 3.47%), PAVE is the best-diversified thematic basket here, and every top name is a profitable industrial or materials company that directly builds US infrastructure. The 0.47% fee is reasonable for a pure-play, $13.3B AUM and $85.7M/day give it ample liquidity, and there is no hidden off-theme sleeve.

**INDA (rank 3, score 8):** INDA offers the broadest diversification in the panel at 171-174 holdings with a top-10 weight of just ~34%, making it the cleanest way to own Indian equity risk without single-stock bets. The 0.61% fee is the main demerit, but for single-country access it is standard, and $249.6M/day of dollar volume plus a tight -0.17% discount/premium show the wrapper tracks its index well.
