Top-3 notes:
- **GRID (9).** IEA's Electricity 2026 says annual grid investment must rise ~50% from today's ~$400bn by 2030 just to keep up with demand, while component prices have more than doubled in ten years and transformer supply is import-dependent. That is a need with no substitute. Wires are the one piece every competing generation technology (gas, nuclear, solar, storage) must share, which is why it outranks every generation theme here (iea.org Electricity 2026 grids chapter and *Building the future transmission grid*, 2025; primary page returned 403, so this rests on iea.org's own snippet).
- **SMH (9).** WSTS's Spring 2026 release (2026-06-02, fetched) has the chip market at $1.51T in 2026 (+90%) and ~$1.9T in 2027 (+27%), with growth in every region. There is no substitute for semiconductors in compute, power or sensing. The −1 is a demand-pace risk (hyperscaler capex outrunning free cash flow by 2027), not a substitution risk.
- **SOXX (9).** Same fact. WSTS also puts memory above $800B and more than half of 2026 revenue, so SOXX's memory-heavier weighting carries the same necessity but more cyclicality. That is why pure-memory DRAM is one point lower (8), not on par.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- Dossier C calls space a "possible break / permanent re-rating" after SpaceX listed. That is about proxy scarcity (cause/basket), not need. Demand for space services is still on a $630bn → $1.8T path to 2035 (WEF/McKinsey), so necessity stays band 7.
- Dossier C frames lithium as a "commodity oversupply cycle". That is supply and price; demand still roughly triples to ~4 Mt LCE by 2035. Sodium-ion (~7% displacement, BNEF) is the real necessity risk and is why LIT is 7, not 8.
- Dossier C cites data-centre moratoria (NY, Texas governor-elect) and post-midterm regulation fears. These constrain supply, not need: CBRE (2026-09-08) shows 1.4% vacancy and >80% preleased despite record construction, so DTCR keeps band 7 +1.
- Dossier C notes JPM's −30% solar-silver demand call. It is consistent with the Silver Institute's PV forecast of −19% in 2026 and is the reason silver sits at band 5 rather than 7. Agree.
- No other necessity-relevant claims in the dossier to dispute.
