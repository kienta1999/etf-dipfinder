Top-3 notes:
- **URNM (10):** The load-bearing fact is that nuclear is the only scalable firm clean power for the AI load: uranium's long-term contract price reached a record ~$98/lb, above its 2007 peak, while spot rose ~10% in 2026 — demand is contracting forward, not shrinking (Betashares, Nov 11, 2026). No substitute generates dispatchable carbon-free power at that scale, and data-center power demand is the new structural buyer.
- **SMH (10):** The load-bearing fact is that every digital system runs on chips and there is no alternative substrate: Nvidia alone guides Q3 FY27 revenue at $108B ±2% (Nvidia release, Oct 28, 2026), and memory is so tight that SK hynix's advanced memory output is effectively committed through 2029 (MarketBeat, Nov 5, 2026). Demand is structurally rising across AI, EVs and digitisation, and efficiency gains trim the growth rate rather than the need.
- **GRID (10):** The load-bearing fact is that electricity has no substitute delivery system: US data-center construction spending reached a record $96.5B annual pace in September 2026, up 90% year on year, and privately-funded data-center spending is ~$4.26B a month, five times the Dec 2022 level (TalkMarkets, Nov 12, 2026). Grid buildout is civilisation-critical infrastructure, not a fashion trade.
