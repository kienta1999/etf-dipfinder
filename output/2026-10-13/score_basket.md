Top-3 notes:
- GLD (9): As a single-commodity grantor trust it is judged on structure, custody, fee and liquidity rather than diversification, and on those terms it is the cleanest basket in the field — allocated physical gold, 0.40% fee and by far the largest asset base. - XAR (9): Modified equal-weighting is the load-bearing fact — top-10 is only about 31-39% with no dominant prime, at a 0.35% fee, unlike cap-weighted ITA where one name exceeds 20%. Sources: Zacks, 2026-08-03/08-26 (0.35%, top-10 30.91%, ~49 holdings) and Zacks, Mar 2026 (top-10 39.2%).
- SLV (8): SLV is theme-pure by construction as a physically backed silver trust, so concentration in one metal is not penalised the way a 22% single stock is. Its deduction is relative — 0.50% fee and a much smaller base than GLD — per Fool, 2026-03-31 (SLV 0.50%, $35.7B vs GLD 0.40%, $155.1B).
