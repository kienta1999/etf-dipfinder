Top-3 notes:
- REMX: The load-bearing fact is the IEA figure, reported 2026-09-22, that demand for the four magnet rare earths has doubled since 2015 and grows another third by 2030, with permanent magnets at about 95% of rare-earth consumption by value. That makes the theme structural, but Tesla-style induction motors and ferrite magnets keep it out of the 10 band.
- URNM: The World Nuclear Association Nuclear Fuel Report 2025 (2025-09-05) projects reactor requirements up 28% by 2030 and more than doubling to 150,000 tU by 2040, while output from today's mines halves over 2030-2040. No other firm, dispatchable low-carbon source scales like-for-like, which is why nuclear scores 8 rather than 7.
- EUAD: At the June 2025 Hague Summit NATO allies committed to 5% of GDP on defence and security by 2035, and the EU ReArm Europe plan envisages mobilising up to 800 billion euros. That is a treaty-level, decade-long demand commitment with no substitute for deterrence, matching the US defence score.

- Crypto-equity (WGMI/BLOK): the dossier leaves the theme unclear; on necessity it is the weakest theme here, because miners pivoting power to AI shows the world can route around crypto mining entirely.
- Space (UFO/ARKX): the dossier leaves the theme unclear; on necessity defence satcom is structural but civil and exploration demand is discretionary and substitutable, so 6, not 7 or above.
