Scarcity intensifying while the equities crater is rotation, not a thesis break; the only offset is REMX's lithium sleeve, hence 9 not 10.
- URNM (9): The load-bearing fact is the uranium term price: Cameco-sourced data put the long-term contract price at $96.50/lb in August and September 2026, above the 2007 peak of $95, with spot near $90/lb in mid-October, even as Cameco itself hit a 52-week low on 2026-10-23 down 42% from its January high. - VNM (9): The load-bearing fact is flow timing, not fundamentals: FTSE Russell's reclassification took effect 2026-09-21 at only a 10% investability weight, with tranche 2 not until 22 March 2027 (+20%, 30% cumulative, LSEG FAQ in dossier D), yet foreigners net-sold roughly $1B in the first month, in 19 of 20 sessions (AsiaDaily, 2026-10-19). Frontier sellers are exiting before the emerging-market buyers are scheduled to arrive, which is a mechanical rotation — capped at 9 because FTSE re-evaluates replication after each tranche.
