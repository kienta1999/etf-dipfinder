Top-3 notes:
- GRID: the grid is the single bottleneck for the whole electrification story — the IEA's Electricity 2026 report says annual grid investment must rise ~50% from today's ~$400B to ~$600B by 2030, with more than 2,500 GW of renewables, storage and large-load projects stuck in connection queues worldwide. No substitute exists for moving electricity from where it is made to where it is used, and data-centre demand alone is set to roughly double to ~1,000 TWh by 2030 (IEA World Energy Outlook 2026, published Nov 19, 2026).
- SMH: semiconductors are the one input every other theme in this panel depends on — Gartner's August 2026 forecast puts global semiconductor revenue at $1.6T in 2026, up 92% year-on-year, with AI semiconductors ~30% of the total and the AI data-centre ecosystem growing from 36.5% of semiconductor revenue in 2026 to over 53% by 2030. There is no substitute for silicon compute, and the WSTS Spring 2026 forecast extends the growth to ~$1.9T in 2027.
- REMX: rare-earth magnets are the one material with no scalable substitute in the applications that matter most — a modern offshore wind turbine carries 600-700 kg of rare-earth magnets, an EV 1-2 kg, and an F-35 ~400 kg of rare earths, with magnet demand growing 8-10% a year and global rare-earth oxide demand projected above 300,000 tonnes by 2026 (MiningSee, 2026). China controls >85% of separation/refining and >90% of magnet production, which is a supply risk, not a demand substitute — the demand itself is structurally rising and cannot be routed around without redesigning the motor, turbine or weapon system.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- Lithium: the memo's "fundamental impairment" (SQM Q4 price warning, oversupply) is a cause/supply point, not a necessity downgrade — lithium demand still grows ~25% in 2026 to 2.08 Mt LCE (Cochilco, Nov 2026) and toward ~7 Mt by 2050, so necessity stays 7.
- Silver: the memo groups silver with gold as a metal hedge, but ~59% of silver demand is industrial and PV thrifting cut solar silver use 19% in 2026 (Silver Institute, Apr 15 2026) — silver's necessity is an industrial-demand story with a visible substitution headwind, scored 6, not a monetary-hedge story.
- Crypto-spot: the memo calls it a "pure flow story with no dated reversal" — that understates it on necessity: the flows themselves just reversed ($4.14B November outflows, worst November since 2018), so demand rests on conditions that may not hold, which is the band-3 definition, not merely an absence of catalyst.
- Otherwise none: I agree with the memo's rotation framing for nuclear, base-metals, gold, defence, grid and space on necessity grounds, and with its watch framing for clean/solar (structurally growing but substitutable, band 7).
