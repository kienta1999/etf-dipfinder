Top-3 notes:
- **COPX (8):** S&P Global's study released 2026-01-08 (press release fetched) projects copper demand rising 50% to 42 Mt by 2040 while production peaks at 33 Mt in 2030, a 10 Mt (25%) shortfall. The IEA's Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 independently has copper adding the most volume of any mineral (~7 Mt to 2040), with a ~25% 2035 deficit (iea.org snippet; the page returns 403). That makes copper the hardest theme in the set to route around.
- **URA (8):** The WNA World Nuclear Fuel Report 2025 (press statement 2025-09-05, fetched) has reactor uranium requirements rising from ~68,920 tU in 2025 to just over 150,000 tU in 2040, with capacity at 746 GWe in the Reference Scenario. The rise is set by reactor fleets, not by any one AI-capex cycle. It stays out of band 10 because gas and renewables-plus-storage remain workable substitutes for firm power.
- **EUAD (8):** NATO's Hague Summit Declaration (2025-06-25, fetched) commits allies to 5% of GDP by 2035, 3.5% of it core defence, with a 2029 review. SIPRI (2026-04-27, fetched) shows European spending already up 14% to $864B in 2025, the main driver of the record $2,887B world total. Need is structurally rising; band 7 rather than 10 only because the platform mix is substitutable.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo:
- **Nuclear:** The memo tags the derating to "AI-capex doubt". On the necessity lens the WNA requirement path (+>100% by 2040, 2025-09-05) does not depend on AI load, so any AI doubt does not dent the 5-10y need.
- **Defense-EU:** The memo cites "de-escalation pricing". The NATO 5%-by-2035 commitment (2025-06-25) is a treaty-level spending path that de-escalation does not undo, and SIPRI shows Europe +14% in 2025. Necessity stays 8.
- **Silver:** The memo calls it "rotation/unclear". There is a structural necessity dent the memo misses: Silver Institute projects PV silver demand −19% in 2026 on thrifting, and total industrial demand at a 4-year low, so the metal's main growth use is being substituted away.
- **Crypto-equity:** The memo says "unclear". CoinShares Q2 2026 (2026-09-15) shows the listed miners are themselves leaving bitcoin mining for AI hosting, which is the clearest route-around in the set. Necessity for the mining theme is 3.
- **Clean/solar:** The memo frames this as "rotation (rates)" only. The IEA cut 2025-30 renewables by 248 GW (US solar −140 GW) after US credits were curtailed by P.L. 119-21 (2025-07-04). The global need is intact (band 7), but the subsidy-dependent leg (PBW's hydrogen names) loses a point.
- **Housing:** The memo says "rotation (rates)". The 5-10y need itself is softer than a pure rate story: JCHS projects net immigration near 0.3M in 2026, and household growth already slowed to 1.1M in 2025, which offsets part of the 4.7M-unit shortage.
