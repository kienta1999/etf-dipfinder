Top-3 notes: SMH — McKinsey's 2026 revision lifts the semiconductor market to $2.3 trillion by 2030 at a 19% CAGR, up from 13%, with data-centre chip revenue rising from $330B in 2025 to $1.2T by 2030. That is the load-bearing fact because it makes chips a structural, not cyclical, necessity with no substitute for AI compute. SOXX — The same McKinsey base case puts leading-edge and memory supply short of demand through 2030, with SK Hynix's chairman citing a wafer deficit over 20% and a shortage lasting to 2030. That supply-demand gap is why SOXX scores in the 10-band rather than merely growing. GRID — The IEA's Electricity 2026 report says over 2,500 GW of renewables, storage and large-load projects are stalled in grid connection queues worldwide and annual grid investment must rise ~50% by 2030. That queue is the load-bearing fact because electricity cannot reach users without the grid, whatever generation is built.

- Grid/infra: PAVE scores 9, not 10 like GRID — PAVE is broader US infrastructure, not the pure grid bottleneck, so it is 10-band minus 1.
