Top-3 notes
- **GLD (8).** FOMC Sep 16 2026 is the nearest, most certain, best-positioned-against event on the board — it carries a Summary of Economic Projections (only Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec do), and the drawdown was *created* by hike odds that ran to ~66% after Warsh's inflation speech and have since slid to roughly 50% on softer retail sales and a slowing labour market. A hold plus a benign dot plot is the surprise, and gold is the cleanest instrument for it; a hike is the symmetric risk, which is why this is an 8 and not a 10.
- **URA (8).** The Prohibiting Russian Uranium Imports Act bans LEU imports outright from Jan 1 2028, Rosatom supplied ~25% of US enriched uranium, and Western enrichment will not backfill before 2030 — utilities buy 12-24 months ahead, so the contracting decisions land inside this window. Annual contracting is still running below the 150 Mlb replacement rate while term price holds a 14-year high of $90-91.50/lb against spot $85.75 (Crux Investor, 2026): the deadline is dated even though the re-rating headline is not.
- **URNM (8).** Same clock, but this is the levered expression — Cameco 21%, SPUT 14%, NexGen 13% — so a single Cameco Q3 print (early Nov 2026) or a Kazatomprom 2027 production cut moves it far more than the diversified peers. The catch is symmetry: that concentration also means one Cameco cost miss re-runs the drawdown, so the catalyst is powerful rather than safe.

Disagreements with the dossier's first-pass memo
- **The memo treats the September FOMC purely as downside** ("if the September meeting hikes, this whole list gets cheaper"). On this lens it is the opposite: a dated, high-probability, dot-plot meeting that the market is positioned hawkishly into is the single best catalyst on the board, and it is why GLD/SLV/XLU/ITB rank as high as they do here.
- **KWEB as #1 candidate does not survive the calendar.** China's policy catalysts are *behind* it — the Fourth Plenum was Oct 2025 and the NPC approved the 15th Five-Year Plan on 2026-03-12. What remains in the window (Singles' Day, BABA's ~Nov 24/Dec 1 print, the December CEWC) mostly re-displays the AI-capex margin compression that caused the dip, with roughly half of Alibaba's ¥380bn commitment still unspent. Catalyst 5.
- **"TAN needs a policy reversal" — the reversal already happened and did nothing.** The DC district court vacated Notice 2025-42 on 2026-06-06, restoring the 5% safe harbor before the Jul 4 deadline. The tape did not care. That is the strongest available evidence that no calendar item reverses this flow.
- **UFO's problem is not only a permanent re-rating — it is a dated supply event.** SPCX's staggered unlocks run through the autumn to a Dec 8 2026 backstop, and S&P rejected fast-track inclusion on 2026-06-04 (12-month seasoning plus GAAP profitability), so the one bullish date is mid-2027 at the earliest.
- **SHLD and XAR are not the same trade on this lens.** SHLD has a specific, near, high-probability vote — the Bundestag's late-November adoption of a 2027 budget already drafted at €109.8bn — aimed squarely at the peace-risk fear that caused its selloff. XAR's $1.14tn FY27 authorization is well telegraphed against a shallow drawdown, so it re-rates less. 7 vs 6.
- **The memo says the uranium pair "hasn't turned yet" and leaves it at that.** The turn has a forcing function with a hard date on it (Jan 1 2028), which is what separates uranium from every other "eventually mean-reverts" name in this list.
