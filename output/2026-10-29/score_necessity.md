Top-3 notes:
- URNM: Uranium is the load-bearing fact because operating reactors have no substitute fuel, and the dossier pins spot uranium at about $90/lb with a long-term contract price of $96.50, above the 2007 peak. That fuel specificity, plus IEA Electricity 2026 (February 2026) projecting nuclear output growth of 2.8% a year to 2030, is why it ranks first rather than on price action.
- NLR: The load-bearing fact is the same reactor-fuel need carried by a broader nuclear basket, with IEA Electricity 2026 (February 2026) projecting renewables plus nuclear to reach 50% of world electricity by 2030. Nuclear generation itself can be substituted by gas and renewables at the margin, which is the one-point cap from band 10.
- URA: The load-bearing fact is again uranium fuel for the existing and expanding reactor fleet, with the dossier citing the same about $90/lb spot and $96.50 long-term price and IEA Electricity 2026 (February 2026) citing nearly 30 GW of new Chinese nuclear capacity in 2026-2030. URA ranks third only because its miner mix is closest to the commodity price, not because the theme need differs from URNM.
