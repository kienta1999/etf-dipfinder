## Fund-specific divergences

- UFO (6) vs ARKX (7): same space theme, different depth and mix — UFO fell ~38% from its ~$68 high in the SpaceX-IPO momentum unwind and is the purer space-momentum vehicle, while ARKX is only -15.3% off its high with defense exposure diluting the unwind. My space search (which included the fund-closure query terms) returned no confirmation of a UFO closure announcement; the documented trigger in what I actually read was the SpaceX-IPO valuation reset, so UFO is scored on that, one point below ARKX.
- URNM/URA (8) vs NLR (7): miners get +1 on the record term price directly contradicting an equity break; NLR stays at band 7 because its broader basket carries the SMR/AI-power de-rating (Oklo, NuScale ~-50% YTD, Holtec IPO pulled).
- PBW (4) vs ICLN (5): PBW's US small/micro-cap clean-tech basket takes the US subsidy repeal and financing shock undiluted; ICLN's global holdings (including Chinese and European names) dilute the same policy cause.
- ARGT: fund-specific amplifier noted but not separately scored — MercadoLibre is 21.40% of ARGT (iShares/INDmoney holdings list found in search) and MELI itself is ~27% off its 2025 peak on profit pressure from logistics investment and competition, so part of ARGT's -10.1% 10-day drop is single-name, on top of the Argentina political de-rating.
