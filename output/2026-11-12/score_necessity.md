Top-3 notes: URNM — The World Nuclear Association's 2025 Fuel Report projects reactor uranium demand doubling from ~68,000t in 2025 to over 150,000t by 2040, with 88 GWe under construction in Oct 2026, the most since 1987. There is no substitute fuel for a light-water reactor, so this demand is locked in for the life of the fleet.
GRID — The IEA's Electricity 2026 (Feb 2026) forecasts global electricity demand growing >3.5% a year to 2030, with annual grid investment needing to rise 50% by 2030 and 2,500 GW of projects stuck in connection queues. The grid is the physical bottleneck of the AI age, and no alternative delivers power from generator to data centre.
SMH — McKinsey's 2026 forecast puts the semiconductor market at $2.3T by 2030 (base case), up from $775B in 2024, with five hyperscalers planning ~$800B of capex in 2026 and ~$1T in 2027. Chips are the substrate of all AI compute, and no competing technology replaces silicon at scale within 5-10 years.
