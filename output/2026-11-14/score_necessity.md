Top-3 notes: REMX — the load-bearing fact is that there is no equal substitute for NdFeB magnets in EV motors, wind turbines and defence systems, and the IEA's 2026 outlook puts China at 91% of refined rare-earth output and 94% of magnet production, so the world cannot route around either the material or the supplier. Magnet rare-earth demand rises about one-third between 2024 and 2030 on IEA current-policy assumptions, which is a structural, policy-driven need rather than a cyclical one. SMH — the load-bearing fact is the WSTS Spring 2026 forecast of a $1.51 trillion semiconductor market in 2026, up 90% year over year, with logic up 37% and memory up ~250% on AI infrastructure demand. Chips are the substrate of AI, EVs, defence and every connected device, and no competing technology replaces silicon at scale in 5-10 years. GRID — the load-bearing fact is the IEA's Electricity 2026 finding that global power demand grows ~3.6% a year through 2030 while over 2,500 GW of projects sit stalled in grid-connection queues, requiring annual grid investment to rise ~50% by 2030 to ~$600bn. A grid that cannot connect new generation or data-centre load is a hard physical bottleneck, not a preference, so the need is civilisation-critical and unsubstitutable.
