That makes grid buildout civilisation-critical rather than merely cyclical, with only permitting/regulatory pace holding it back from a 10.
- SOXX (9): The single load-bearing fact is the scale and durability of AI compute demand — hyperscaler capex is projected at $1.2T in 2027 per Goldman Sachs (cited in the dossier), and semiconductors are the one input with no substitute for that buildout. The -1 from 10 is cyclicality in memory, PC and legacy segments, which the dossier itself shows falling hardest in the unwind.
- SMH (9): The single load-bearing fact is the same as SOXX — hyperscaler capex projected at $1.2T in 2027 per Goldman Sachs (cited in the dossier) makes leading-edge chip demand structurally rising, and there is no non-silicon route to AI compute in the 5–10y window. The -1 from 10 is the same cyclical memory/legacy exposure that dragged the basket down in October.
