Top-3 notes:
- XLU (10): Electricity demand is the load-bearing fact — IEA Electricity 2026 (Feb 2026) puts global demand growth at over 3.5% a year to 2030, an increment equal to more than two European Unions. There is no substitute for electricity itself in 5-10 years, so this is the only clean band-10 theme alongside the grid that carries it.
- GRID (10): The bottleneck fact is the queue — IEA Electricity 2026 (Feb 2026) reports over 2,500 GW of renewables, storage and large-load projects stalled in connection queues and requires annual grid investment to rise about 50% by 2030 to about $600bn. Generation without transmission delivers nothing, which is why GRID scores with utilities rather than with the generation themes at 7.
- XHB (9): The shortage fact is structural, not cyclical sentiment — Realtor.com's 2026 Housing Supply Gap Report puts the 2025 US gap at 4.03 million homes, up from 3.8 million in 2024, after more than a decade of underbuilding. It is held one point below band 10 only because the funds own new-build, which renting and the existing stock partly substitute for.
